About

Laundry Day Laundromat is a auto shop based in Seattle, WA. The work is coin-operated laundries and drycleaners for the people and businesses of this area, done locally, by people who live with the reputation it earns them.

The approach here is straightforward. Things are explained in plain words, arrangements are kept, and people are treated the way the people here would want their own family treated.
