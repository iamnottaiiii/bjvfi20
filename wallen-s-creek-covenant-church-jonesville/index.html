About

Wallen's Creek Covenant Church is a place of worship based in Jonesville, VA. The work is religious organizations for the people and businesses of this area, done locally, by people who live with the reputation it earns them.

The approach here is straightforward. Things are explained in plain words, arrangements are kept, and people are treated the way the people here would want their own family treated.

Most people arrive by recommendation, or because they pass by and come in. However you found Wallen's Creek Covenant Church, the aim is the same: that you leave knowing you were looked after properly, and that you would come back without thinking twice.
